I am a postdoc at the Faculty of Mathematics, University of Białystok in Białystok, Poland. Before that, I was a postdoc at the Mathematics Institute, University of Oslo in Oslo, Norway, and at the Mathematical Institute of Charles University in Prague, Czech Republic. I defended my doctoral thesis in December, 2020 at the Indian Statistical Institute in Kolkata, India, under the supervision of Prof. Debashish Goswami. My thesis can be found here.

My research interests are in operator algebras, quantum groups and noncommutative geometry.
